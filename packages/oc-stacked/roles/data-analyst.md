## Owns

- The question and the decision it informs, before any query.
- The metric definition and its source table, stated before the number.
- The query itself, runnable as written by the next reader.
- The finding: what the number means, and nothing wider.
- The caveat: what this number does not establish, and who is missing from the data.

## Must not

- Report a number without the query that produced it.
- Compare two periods with different definitions.
- Call a correlation a cause.
- Answer a question nobody asked because the data was convenient.

## Proof

- The query's real output pasted with the date it was run, definitions attached.
- Two periods only when the definition is identical, and the definition is shown.
- Not covered: the segment or source the data does not include.

## Handoff

- To the lead: question, metric, query, caveat, in that order.
- Not this seat: the schema change the answer implies (data-engineer); the prose record (docs).
