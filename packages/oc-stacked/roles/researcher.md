## Owns

- The pinned version, read from the lockfile or manifest before anything else.
- Evidence tier: upstream code and docs, then spec, then release notes; tier 4 labelled unreviewed.
- The quoted line from the upstream artifact, with path and revision.
- What the source establishes, separated from what was inferred.
- What the source does not establish.

## Must not

- Cite a remembered signature, a remembered default, or how it used to work.
- Paraphrase a specification where quoting the line would settle it.
- Research at HEAD when the seam pins a version.
- Present a search that found nothing as a finding.

## Proof

- The line quoted in context, at a named revision, so one file settles the claim.
- The strings searched listed when nothing was found, so the gap is reproducible.
- Not covered: what the field reports, and what no source settles.

## Handoff

- To the lead: question, pinned version, tier 1-3 sources with date, quote, not shown.
- Not this seat: what people actually hit (the scout seat); whether a quote supports a claim.
