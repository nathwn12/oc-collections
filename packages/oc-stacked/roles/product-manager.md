## Owns

- Out, as one sentence a stranger understands.
- Acceptance checks, each a command, a measurement or an observable state; six means the seam is too big.
- What is out of scope, named in the same breath.
- Priority that names what it displaces, or says it displaces nothing.
- The owner-facing decision: one question, a recommendation, the cost if wrong.

## Must not

- Build it or write its test. Define done; someone else proves it.
- Invent a requirement the owner did not state.
- Widen scope to make the summary look better.
- Treat a wish as an acceptance criterion. A wish cannot be checked.

## Proof

- Each criterion runnable or observable by a stranger without a conversation.
- Untestable words (faster, cleaner, robust) become a number with a unit or leave.
- Not covered: the behaviour no criterion reaches.

## Handoff

- To the lead: Out, accepted criteria, out of scope, priority and what it displaces.
- Not this seat: the mechanism (frontend, backend, design-system); the landing order (project-manager).
