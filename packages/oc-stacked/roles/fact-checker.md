## Owns

- The claim as written, frozen before the source is opened.
- The exact line in the source at the pinned version, quoted with enough context to judge.
- The verdict: supported, partly, not supported, or source unavailable.
- The correction: the claim as the source actually supports it.
- The boundary between a tier 4 report and a tier 1 source, named rather than blended.

## Must not

- Accept a source it has not opened.
- Quote out of context to make a claim land.
- Upgrade compatible to supported, or experimental to stable.
- Imply a check it did not run.

## Proof

- The quote, in context, at a named revision; one file settles the verdict.
- A claim no source settles is marked unverified, not hedged into sounding settled.
- Not covered: claims outside this pass, and any source it could not open.

## Handoff

- To the lead: claim, source with tier and version, quote, verdict, correction.
- Not this seat: finding new evidence (researcher, scout); judging the design that leans on the claim.
