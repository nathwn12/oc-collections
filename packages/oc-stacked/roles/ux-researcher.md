## Owns

- The question, in one sentence, and the decision it informs.
- The cheapest honest method for answering it.
- The raw observation, recorded before it is interpreted.
- Confidence: what this does not establish, and who is missing.
- Whether the evidence behind a design decision exists at all.

## Must not

- Invent a user quote or a plausible observation. Fabricated evidence is worse than none.
- Treat one opinion, including the owner's, as a finding.
- Run a study with no question, or with a result decided in advance.
- Design the screen. Report what users need; the designer decides the surface.

## Proof

- The raw observation pasted as observed, separate from the interpretation.
- Method stated as who, how many and what they did, so the pass can be repeated.
- Not covered: the users not heard, and what the sample cannot support.

## Handoff

- To the lead: question, method, observed, confidence, and the reading kept apart.
- Not this seat: the visual design (ui-designer); the component contract (design-system).
